# Sample blog posts — ready to paste

Six fashion articles for the **News** blog (`/blogs/news`). Shopify themes can't create posts, so add them in **Shopify admin > Online Store > Blog posts > Create blog post**:

1. **Title** — copy the title.
2. **Content** — click the **Show HTML** button (`<>`) in the editor toolbar and paste the HTML block.
3. **Excerpt** — "Add excerpt", paste the excerpt (shown on the blog cards).
4. **Image** — upload, or pick from **Select existing** (the file names below are already in Content > Files).
5. **Tags** — type the tags (the first tag is the pill on the card; tags become the topic filters on the blog page).
6. **Blog** — News. **Visibility** — Visible. Save.

Once the blog has posts, turn off **Theme editor > Blogs > Blog posts > "Show sample posts while the blog is empty"** (it only shows while the blog is empty anyway).

This file is not part of the theme (Shopify ignores files outside the theme folders).

## 1. Five easy pieces for a smarter wardrobe

- **Tags:** Style, Basics
- **Image:** `photo-1516762689617-e1cffcef479d_388c7393-ee09-40fb-8858-f840023d5676.jpg`
- **Excerpt:** Build a closet that works every morning with five versatile basics that pair with everything you own.

```html
<p>Getting dressed should take minutes, not a crisis. The secret isn't owning more — it's owning pieces that work together. These five basics are the backbone of a wardrobe that simply works.</p>
<h2>1. A heavyweight tee</h2>
<p>A thick cotton tee holds its shape, layers under jackets and looks sharp on its own. Choose a neutral — black, cream or grey — and buy two.</p>
<h2>2. Straight-leg denim</h2>
<p>Not too slim, not too wide. A straight leg works with sneakers, boots and loafers, and it never looks dated.</p>
<h2>3. An oversized hoodie</h2>
<p>The off-duty hero. Wear it with denim for errands or throw it under a coat when the temperature drops.</p>
<h2>4. A light jacket</h2>
<p>A denim jacket or a twill overshirt bridges the seasons. It's the piece that makes a simple outfit look finished.</p>
<h2>5. Clean sneakers</h2>
<p>White or off-white, low-profile, easy to clean. They dress down tailoring and dress up joggers.</p>
<blockquote>Buy fewer things, but choose them well — and wear them often.</blockquote>
<p>Start with what you already own, fill the gaps one piece at a time, and you'll find you reach for the same favourites again and again. That's the point.</p>
```

## 2. How to style an oversized hoodie

- **Tags:** Trends, Hoodies
- **Image:** `photo-1529139574466-a303027c1d8b_07fb9522-30e0-4861-a641-de148de545dc.jpg`
- **Excerpt:** Three looks, one hoodie: from Saturday errands to dinner plans, here's how to make the comfiest piece you own work harder.

```html
<p>The oversized hoodie is the most comfortable thing in your closet — and with a little styling, one of the most versatile. Here are three ways to wear it.</p>
<h2>Look 1: Weekend easy</h2>
<p>Pair it with relaxed cargo pants and chunky sneakers. Push the sleeves up slightly so the proportions feel intentional, not sloppy.</p>
<h2>Look 2: Smart casual</h2>
<p>Layer it under a structured jacket or a long coat. The contrast between soft and tailored is what makes this look work. Finish with straight-leg trousers and leather shoes.</p>
<h2>Look 3: Dinner ready</h2>
<p>Go tonal: a cream hoodie with cream trousers, or all black. Add a watch and a simple chain — small details make a casual piece feel considered.</p>
<h2>Getting the fit right</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Shoulders:</strong> the seam should drop just past your shoulder, not halfway down your arm.</li>
<li><strong>Length:</strong> it should end around the hip — longer reads as a dress, shorter looks shrunk.</li>
<li><strong>Fabric:</strong> heavier fleece holds its shape and drapes better than thin jersey.</li>
</ul>
<p>Not sure about your size? Our team is happy to help — just send us a message.</p>
```

## 3. The denim guide: fits that flatter

- **Tags:** Guides, Denim
- **Image:** `photo-1517841905240-472988babdf9_6a381994-0630-4ecf-941b-9d6fb3ba522f.jpg`
- **Excerpt:** Straight, relaxed or wide-leg? A simple guide to finding the denim cut that suits you — and how to wear each one.

```html
<p>Few things change an outfit as much as the cut of your jeans. Here's a quick guide to the three fits we get asked about most.</p>
<h2>Straight leg</h2>
<p>The safest bet. It falls in a clean line from hip to hem, works on almost every body shape and pairs with anything. If you only own one pair, make it this one.</p>
<h2>Relaxed fit</h2>
<p>A little more room through the thigh and a tapered or straight leg. Comfortable all day and great with chunky sneakers or boots.</p>
<h2>Wide leg</h2>
<p>Statement-making and surprisingly easy to wear. Balance the volume with a fitted top or a tucked-in tee, and add a shoe with some height.</p>
<h2>Rise and length</h2>
<p>A mid or high rise flatters most figures and keeps tops neatly tucked. For length, aim for the hem to just touch the top of your shoe — or roll a clean single cuff.</p>
<blockquote>The best jeans are the ones you forget you're wearing.</blockquote>
<h2>Caring for denim</h2>
<p>Wash inside out, in cold water, and as rarely as you can. Hang to dry. Your denim will keep its colour and shape for years.</p>
```

## 4. Caring for knits so they last for years

- **Tags:** Care, Knitwear
- **Image:** `photo-1490481651871-ab68de25d43d_e0a6c406-4da5-4a87-9a71-91f0f3f9e93f.jpg`
- **Excerpt:** Wash less, fold more and say goodbye to pilling: simple habits that keep your favourite knits looking new.

```html
<p>A good sweater can last a decade — if you treat it right. These simple habits make all the difference.</p>
<h2>Wash less</h2>
<p>Knits rarely need washing after every wear. Air them out overnight instead. When they do need a wash, use cold water and a wool-friendly detergent.</p>
<h2>Never hang, always fold</h2>
<p>Hangers stretch the shoulders out of shape. Fold your knits and store them flat, with the heaviest at the bottom of the pile.</p>
<h2>Dry flat</h2>
<p>Gently press out water with a towel — never wring — then lay the sweater flat on a dry towel, reshaping it as it dries.</p>
<h2>Deal with pilling</h2>
<p>Small bobbles are normal where fabric rubs. A fabric comb or shaver removes them in minutes and makes a sweater look new again.</p>
<ul>
<li>Store knits with cedar blocks to keep moths away.</li>
<li>Give them a rest day between wears.</li>
<li>Repair small holes early, before they grow.</li>
</ul>
<p>Look after your knits, and they'll look after you for many winters to come.</p>
```

## 5. Weekend layers for changing weather

- **Tags:** Style, Outerwear
- **Image:** `photo-1525507119028-ed4c629a60a3_562f9089-89e4-459a-acf0-718b9984cb98.jpg`
- **Excerpt:** Sunny morning, chilly afternoon, rain by evening? Smart layering keeps up when the forecast doesn't.

```html
<p>In-between weather calls for layers you can add and remove without losing the look. Here's our simple three-layer formula.</p>
<h2>Base layer</h2>
<p>Start light and breathable: a cotton tee or a fine knit. It should look good on its own when the sun comes out.</p>
<h2>Middle layer</h2>
<p>Add warmth with an overshirt, a cardigan or a hoodie. This is the layer that gives an outfit its personality.</p>
<h2>Outer layer</h2>
<p>A light jacket — denim, a puffer vest or a water-resistant shell — handles wind and drizzle and folds into a bag when you don't need it.</p>
<blockquote>Dress for the coldest part of your day, and make every layer worth showing.</blockquote>
<h2>Keep the palette calm</h2>
<p>Layers look best when they share a colour family. Mix creams, stones, olives and black, and everything will go with everything.</p>
```

## 6. Accessories that finish a look

- **Tags:** Trends, Accessories
- **Image:** `photo-1516762689617-e1cffcef479d_388c7393-ee09-40fb-8858-f840023d5676.jpg`
- **Excerpt:** Belts, bags and watches that pull an outfit together — the small details that make simple clothes look considered.

```html
<p>Accessories are the quickest way to make a simple outfit feel finished. You don't need many — just a few that work hard.</p>
<h2>A leather belt</h2>
<p>Choose one in black and one in brown, with a simple buckle. Match your belt to your shoes and you'll always look put together.</p>
<h2>A minimal watch</h2>
<p>A clean dial and a leather or mesh strap works from the office to the weekend. It's the one piece of jewellery almost everyone wears daily.</p>
<h2>An everyday bag</h2>
<p>A structured tote or a crossbody bag carries the essentials and anchors the outfit. Neutral colours go with the most.</p>
<h2>Sunglasses</h2>
<p>A classic shape — round, rectangular or tortoiseshell — adds instant polish, even with a tee and jeans.</p>
<p>Start with one piece, wear it with everything for a week, and notice how often people ask where it's from.</p>
```
